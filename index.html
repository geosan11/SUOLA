FAQ

Questions travelers ask us.

Does Suola own the airlines, cargo planes, or transfer cars?
No. We are an on-the-ground concierge and connector. We coordinate, negotiate, and verify your bookings across licensed commercial airlines (Air Peace, Ibom, Overland), freight handlers, and private chauffeurs. The physical flights and transportation are delivered by those licensed providers under their standard conditions.
How do payments work?
We provide a 100% transparent, final price on WhatsApp before you pay. You can settle via verified bank transfer or physically at our MM2 First Floor desk via POS or cash. Official receipts and partner itineraries are issued immediately.
What happens if an airline delays or cancels my flight?
Because we are physically on the departure floor, we liaise directly with airline duty managers. If one airline cancels, we immediately present flight options on competing carriers to get you to your destination without getting stranded.
How fast does air cargo travel?
Parcels dropped at our MM2 First Floor desk before 10:00 AM generally travel on scheduled afternoon flights, arriving in Abuja, Port Harcourt, or Kano the very same day.

Have another question about your flight or luggage?

Our MM2 floor team responds in real time during airport flight hours (06:00 – 21:00).
