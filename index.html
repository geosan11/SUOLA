About Suola Logistics

Suola Logistics is a trusted provider of aviation and logistics services based at MM2 Local Airport (Murtala Muhammed Airport Terminal 2), First Floor, Departure Hall, Lagos.

Led by Chief Executive Officer Dolapo Adeniyi, we specialize in making travel and cargo movement seamless for individuals and businesses. From flight ticketing to full airport support, cargo handling, car hiring and hotel reservations — we handle it all.

Visit us at the airport or contact us anytime. We’re committed to reliable, professional service that gets you and your goods where they need to go.
